About Mental Go

A simple space to check in with yourself.

Mental Go is a personal wellness platform designed to make everyday self-care easier to organize.

Whether you want to reflect on your mood, build a better routine, take a short breathing break or simply write down your thoughts, Mental Go gives you one place to do it.
